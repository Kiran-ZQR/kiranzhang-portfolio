OverviewThe challengeThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Two journeys, one coherent conversation.

Working within the existing plugin experience and design standards, I completed the comment-related screens and interaction states for articles and immersive video.

02The challenge

Participation needs context; replies need a way back.

Users should be able to identify what they are responding to, distinguish a comment from a reply, and return to the relevant discussion after receiving a message.

03The experience

Join the discussion without losing the content.

Video comments open alongside the viewing context. Article comments preserve the relationship to the original story. A message list then connects a reply to its destination.

Deliver the states around the main flow.
