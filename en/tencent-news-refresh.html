A fresh start, without leaving the feed.

Bring pull-to-refresh to the first immersive video, giving users a way forward when the opening recommendation misses their interests.

IN THIS CASE

OverviewThe challengeDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Make the first recommendation recoverable.

The article landing page already supports pull-to-refresh. In the immersive feed, users could only accept the opening recommendation. The same plugin therefore offered different levels of control.

02The challenge

A content mismatch had no recovery path.

The issue was not simply the absence of a button. Users needed a familiar way to ask for something different while staying in the viewing context.

03Design approach

Reuse the gesture. Restrict the trigger.

Keep the familiar pull-down action, make it available only on the first item, and provide brief feedback while content changes.

04The experience

One clear recovery loop.

The user expresses an intent to refresh, receives feedback, and continues with a new recommendation.

05Outcome

A small interaction with a clear purpose.

The delivered proposal adds a recovery path using a familiar gesture. The first-item boundary and short refresh feedback keep that action connected to the existing viewing flow.
