When the first item is uninteresting, users lack a way to refresh on their own

The issue was not simply the absence of a button. Users needed a familiar way to ask for something different while staying in the viewing context.

Design notes & supporting detail+

01 / CONTEXT

Two content formats coexist in the plugin: the landing page supports pull-to-refresh, but the first immersive video only offers the current recommendation.

Plugin landing page

01 Pull-to-refresh available
EXPERIENCE GAP

Users expect pulling down to refresh; that sense of control disappears in the immersive feed.

Plugin immersive video screen

02 Only the current recommendation

The issue is not just a missing refresh action, but no recovery path after a mismatch

Make refresh a lightweight correction , without breaking immersion

Keep the familiar pull-down action, make it available only on the first item, and provide brief feedback while content changes.

Familiar

Reuse the existing pull-down mental model.

Controlled

Continuous

02 / DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Reuse a familiar gesture to offer a discoverable, reversible and unobtrusive way to refresh the first item.
