SADRA

A quieter bridge to connection.

An AI companion concept combining wearable interaction, personal support and a community layer for people experiencing social anxiety.

IN THIS CASE

OverviewThe challengeDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Design a companion around the person, not just a chat window.

SADRA explores how a personal AI assistant and a wearable object could make support feel available in everyday situations. The concept connects conversation, physical interaction and community.

02The challenge

Support can be difficult to access or initiate.

The research boards discuss financial and time costs, uncertainty about personal feelings, and the desire for connection. They include an interview, questionnaire work and comparisons with existing AI-supported services.

03Design approach

Start with awareness, then offer different ways to connect.

The concept is organised around personal setup, active requests, responses to physical signals and community sharing. This allows the interaction to extend beyond an always-open conversation.

04The experience

An assistant, a physical touchpoint and a community.

The wearable provides a tangible interaction object; the assistant supports conversation; the community offers another form of connection. The system and hardware boards show how these parts were conceived together.

05Outcome

A product concept and prototype direction.

The project brings research, a user journey, a wearable object and prototype testing into one product direction. The next design question is how people initiate, understand and control support in everyday situations.
