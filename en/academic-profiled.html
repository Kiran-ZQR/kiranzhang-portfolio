IN THIS CASE

OverviewThe challengeDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Make personal development more visible.

PROFILED explores a quality-oriented education system with complementary equipment, using a spatial-computing interface inspired by Apple Vision Pro. It connects individual courses, group work and challenge activities.

02The challenge

Scores reveal only part of a student’s development.

The project research addresses academic pressure, limited self-understanding and restricted opportunities to explore interests. The boards include interviews, questionnaire analysis and education case studies.

03Design approach

Organise the system around different learning modes.

The information architecture separates the personal interface, course projects, collaboration, challenges and settings. Low-fidelity work helped test how those areas should connect.

04The experience

Move between personal, shared and challenge spaces.

My recorded contribution focused on the interaction experience, the proposed design, modelling and layout of the actual UI/UX process. The spatial concept brings progress and activities into one navigable environment.

05Outcome

Prototype feedback points to a clearer hierarchy.

The documented test feedback highlights recognisable main functions alongside colour overload and inconsistent interface divisions. That makes simplification and clearer shared-space grouping concrete directions for refinement.
