OverviewThe challengeDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Apply a standard consistently, page by page.

The team already had light/dark colour standards. My work mapped the product-supplied Excel values into Light and Dark modes in Figma Variables and completed editable page designs.

02The challenge

Theme adaptation is more than changing the background.

Headlines, supporting text, toolbars, video surroundings and branded briefing headers must retain their information relationships in both modes.

03Design approach

Map, redraw, then review in context.

The work followed existing standards rather than creating a new design system.
