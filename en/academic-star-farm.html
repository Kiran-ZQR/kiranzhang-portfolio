OverviewThe challengeDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Build a tangible way into a digital world.

Star Farm explores interactive products that combine physical activity and multisensory feedback. The original material records school visits, product iterations and field testing.

02The challenge

Interaction must fit different physical and attention needs.

Observation and conversations with staff at Lok Yi School informed the project. The documented needs include accessible physical activity, concentration and multisensory training.

03Design approach

Let the school context shape the product.

The project moved from site visits and concept definition to initial artwork, product testing and a later school field test. The design connects physical objects with digital animation and feedback.

04The experience

A responsive environment around a physical action.

Pressure-sensitive forms connect gripping and releasing with visual plant growth. Tactile variety, animation and sound support an approachable play loop. My recorded contribution covered product technology design and user interface design within the team.

05Outcome

An interaction shaped through school visits and testing.

School visits, prototype iterations and field-test records create a concrete basis for refinement. The next design focus is how physical input, visual response and session pacing fit the activities guided by school staff.
