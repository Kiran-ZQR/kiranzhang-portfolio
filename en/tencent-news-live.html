IN THIS CASE

OverviewDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

One focused change in the host room.

A livestream combines the video, host information and a stream of updates. Important host content needs to remain discoverable when viewers arrive through a shared page.

02Design approach

Separate a pinned update from the stream.

The design gives pinned content a stable, recognisable position while retaining the host identity, follow entry and surrounding messages.

03The experience

Keep the video and its context together.

The delivered states show the host-room layout across app and shared-page contexts. Text and media updates remain part of the same discussion structure.

  3. 03

04Outcome

A concise UI delivery.

The delivered screens cover pinned text and media updates in the host room, including the shared-page context. The key outcome is a recognisable place for important host content within an ongoing livestream.
