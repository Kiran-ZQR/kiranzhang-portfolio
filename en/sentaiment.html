OverviewDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Explore emotion through interaction, not a conventional interface.

SentAIment investigates how natural language, gesture and AI-generated imagery can contribute to an expressive installation. The project connects prompts and visual output with physical interaction.

02Design approach

Connect sensing, generation and response.

The implementation boards document TouchDesigner and Python, image generation, gesture tracking and an Arduino/TGAM input path. These become parts of an audiovisual experiment rather than separate tools.

  1. 01

    Capture an input

  2. 02

    Shape a prompt or transformation

  3. 03

    Return a visual response

03The experience

Make the response spatial and visible.

The prototype turns generated imagery into a responsive visual environment. Installation photographs show participants encountering and influencing that environment.

04Outcome

An experiential prototype of human–AI expression.

The installation joins input, prompts and visual response in one experiential prototype. It creates a basis for exploring how participants interpret feedback, express themselves and develop a sense of agency within the environment.
