WECHAT PLUGIN / CONTENT DISCOVERY

Tencent News

Show there is more to watch.

Replace an obstructive tutorial cue with a preview-led swipe-up invitation on the first video.

IN THIS CASE

OverviewThe challengeDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

A native control sent the wrong signal.

WeChat’s bottom controls could suggest that the page could not be swiped. Some users watched only the first video and left, without discovering the recommendation stream.

02The challenge

Instruction competed with the content.

The existing tutorial-style guide covered bottom information while communicating little about the next item. A gesture cue alone did not provide a reason to continue.

03Design approach

Pair the action with a reason to act.

Retain the native swipe gesture, remove the prominent blue feedback strip, and introduce information about the next video after a five-second pause.

  1. 01

    Watch for five seconds

  2. 02

    Preview the next item

  3. 03

    Swipe up or dismiss

04The experience

A next-content preview becomes the invitation.

The guide combines an upward cue, the next cover and its information. Users can understand what happens next and dismiss the cue voluntarily.

05Outcome

Read interaction gains alongside neutral consumption results.

The recorded launch data shows increases in share and like clicks, while consumption measures did not show a significant change.
