OverviewThe challengeThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Complete the interface around a personalised service.

My contribution covered UI production and missing states for the briefing flow, plus the radio icon for the third item in the home page’s bottom navigation.

02The challenge

Generated content is not yet a confirmed subscription.

Users need to know whether the system is still generating, whether they are viewing a preview, and when their preferences have actually been saved.

03The experience

Separate generation, confirmation and completion.

The main flow gives each stage its own feedback and next action. Preferences keep naming, visibility, saving and unsubscribing together after setup.

04Outcome

A radio icon adopted in a later version.

I drew the bottom-navigation radio icon in 2025, when the entry had not launched. In 2026, I observed the live Radio entry using my concept with changes to its outline and internal mark. The original label was “Listen”; the later label is “Radio”.

The icon contribution concerns the bottom navigation, not the top briefing entry. Original and later-release designs are shown separately; later refinements are not claimed as my work.
