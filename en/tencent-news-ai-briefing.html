01 / START CUSTOMISING

Delivered flow screens: the briefing includes an AI customisation entry. Discovery separates followed and featured briefings and offers “Customise my AI briefing”. Users can browse existing content or start customising directly.

UI delivery focus

Differentiate “Following” from “Follow” so existing subscriptions and new content are easy to distinguish; keep the customisation entry clear and independent.

02 / GENERATE & CONFIRM

Separate generation feedback, result confirmation and the next destination. Users can understand progress and the subscription commitment before choosing “Complete customisation”.

01 Generating

Show thinking and output progress, while keeping a stop action available.

02 Confirm customisation

Present the result and explain the ongoing service before offering “Complete customisation”.

03 Complete & continue

Confirm completion and use “Take a look” to open the personalised briefing.

Distinct states

Generated result → User confirmation → Customisation complete
Separate content generation from subscription completion so a preview is not mistaken for saved preferences.

03 / MANAGE SUBSCRIPTIONS

The personalised briefing supports daily reading. Preferences bring naming, visibility and unsubscribe actions together so users can recognise and revise what they created.

Each setting has a clear purpose

The name identifies the briefing; “Only visible to me” controls visibility. Save applies edits, while Unsubscribe ends the subscription.
