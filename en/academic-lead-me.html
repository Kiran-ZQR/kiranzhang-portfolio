IN THIS CASE

OverviewThe challengeDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Help adults create material for a specific child and situation.

Lead Me supports the people who prepare educational picture books: parents, teachers and training collaborators. The platform aims to make content simpler to create and more flexible to adapt.

02The challenge

Generic materials are difficult to personalise.

The project materials describe time-consuming teaching-material customisation, recurring training needs and differences in children’s preferences. The design opportunity is a creation workflow that adults can review and adapt.

03Design approach

Keep creation connected to a real learning scenario.

The example material uses a hand-washing story before making chocolate. A concrete sequence helps connect generated illustration, narrative and audio to an everyday activity.

  1. 01

    Choose a learning situation

  2. 02

    Create and customise the story

  3. 03

    Review and use the material

04The experience

Combine illustration, narrative and audio.

The demonstrated picture book presents steps through images, text and audio. As a team project, Lead Me brings these content modes into a creation platform for the adults preparing the material.

05Outcome

A platform direction with further collaboration planned.

The project includes a prototype and a future collaboration plan with Hello Cocoa around vocational-skills materials. The documented next step is to understand employee training guidelines and customise picture-book guides around those tasks.
