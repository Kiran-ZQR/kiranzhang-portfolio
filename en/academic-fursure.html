IN THIS CASE

OverviewThe challengeDesign approachThe experienceOutcome
01Overview

Move from watching animals to understanding them.

FurSure explores digital interactions for zoo visitors, especially families with children. The focus is bird behaviour, respectful interaction and a more engaged visit.

02The challenge

Visitors can misread what they see.

The boards connect stereotyped animal behaviour, visitor misunderstanding and the conditions of a zoo visit. Questionnaire work, a keeper interview and site analysis informed the opportunity.

03Design approach

Build a learning loop through the visit.

The experience combines an entrance prompt, a character-led premise, AR collection, physical exploration and a short knowledge check. The game gives visitors a reason to notice their surroundings.

  1. 01

    Enter and meet the character

  2. 02

    Explore and collect through AR

  3. 03

04The experience

Connect the digital action to the real environment.

A map supports orientation. AR encounters and collection cards link observation to play, while quizzes and progression carry learning beyond the first interaction.

05Outcome

A connected AR game prototype.

The project combines a game structure, wireframes, character modelling and working interaction experiments. A next evaluation can observe whether visitors understand the tasks, connect them to bird behaviour and continue through the visit.
