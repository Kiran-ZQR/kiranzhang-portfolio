# Five-element visual refresh

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original assets: `assets/elements-v2/{wood,fire,earth,metal,water}.png`. Each element was independently generated. Existing homepage letter assets were not changed.

Shared prompt: Create one square flat graphic textile pattern asset for ELEMENT, inspired by cellular automata / metaball-grid design posters. PALETTE. Coarse deliberate 20 by 20 underlying grid, crisp flat shapes, groups of joined squares with subtly softened outer corners, large negative spaces and tiny contrasting registration dots in some empty cells, asymmetric bold graphic composition. Screen-print-like solid inks but no texture noise, no gradients, no 3D, no lighting, no borders, no typography, no labels, no literal element icons, no photorealism. Full bleed. Few strong colors, high contrast, NOT tiny rainbow mosaic confetti. This will be a base texture for a living woven fabric.

- wood: muted slate teal #4d7075 ground with lemon yellow #fff252 connected growing branching right-angle square clusters, accents magenta #c83c9d
- fire: brick red #c9302b ground with dark burgundy #440d09 rising stepped cellular flames and small warm ivory #eee9d5 cross clusters
- earth: ochre #ac8b40 ground with lemon yellow #fff252 connected cellular lattice islands and tiny magenta #c83c9d pinpoints
- metal: dark burgundy #440d09 ground with warm ivory #eee9d5 clean cross-shaped and square lattice clusters, a magenta #c83c9d strip
- water: pale cyan #91d6df ground with dark burgundy #440d09 flowing connected square meanders and tiny ochre #ac8b40 and lemon yellow dots

The five colors are a contemporary reference-led interpretation, not a claim about traditional five-element color conventions. The original B3/S23 simulation remains unchanged; the bitmap is the graphic underlay, and living cells are drawn over it.
